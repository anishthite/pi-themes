Decisions

IDChoice · Rationale
D-001Use Pi's built-in light/dark setting. Pi handles terminal appearance changes without an extension watcher.
D-002Poll AppleInterfaceStyle with launchd. macOS exposes no simple per-user appearance-change hook for this script.

Tradeoffs

IDPicked over Rejected · Reason

T-002GitHub palette over the terminal default colors. Match Pi's selected theme roles.
